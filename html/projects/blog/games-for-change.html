Games for Change Festival

OVERVIEW

This year I had the incredible opportunity to attend the Games for Change Festival in New York City! It took place July 21-22 and brought together creators, researchers, designers and developers together to talk about play and how it fosters experimentation, collaboration and creative exploration. I watched as many conferences and keynotes as I could, took part in some workshops and playtested a lot of games displayed in the Arcade. It was a great chance to talk to fellow developers and I had a lot of fun!

Link to the festival's website: https://festival.gamesforchange.org/
