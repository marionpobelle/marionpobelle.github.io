Forum des Images October 2026: Mirage Miracle Quest Presentation

OVERVIEW

The exhibition series “Be Beautiful and Shut Up! Delphine Seyrig: Portrait of a Free Icon” took place from September 24 to November 1 2026, at the Forum des images in Paris.

I had the opportunity to represent Toe Bean Club by giving a presentation alongside the creative director on Toe Bean Club's first game, Mirage Miracle Quest. The video game industry faces challenges similar to those in the film industry, particularly regarding the role of women. While the industry, like all others, strives to marginalize them, independent studios are breaking free from these expectations and creating original games with more diverse teams.
